Research theme · WP2

River & estuary dynamics

Fast, accurate models of flows over erodible beds, coupled with groundwater and sediment transport.

The challenge

Rivers and estuaries are complex, interconnected systems shaped by physical, chemical and biological processes. Managing them means monitoring water levels, flow rates, sediment and water quality, and relying on numerical tools that can keep up with real-time measurements. Today, river flow, sediment transport and subsurface flow are usually simulated with separate, uncoupled models.

Our approach

RESCUER builds multi-physics shallow-water models that bring these processes together on unstructured meshes:

  • surface flows over erodible beds, including bedload transport and bed erosion;
  • coupling between surface water and variably saturated subsurface (porous) flow, extended to a moving bed;
  • dry and wet granular flows;
  • waves and currents in rivers and estuaries with complex geometries.

Recent advances in numerical techniques and GPU implementations make it possible to run such integrated models efficiently.

Why it matters

Integrated models give a more complete picture of how rivers and estuaries respond to floods, droughts and human interventions, and support better day-to-day management.
