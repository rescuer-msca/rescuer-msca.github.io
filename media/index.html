About RESCUER, in short

RESCUER (Resilient Solutions for Coastal, Urban, Estuarine and Riverine Environments) is a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network funded by the European Union under Horizon Europe (grant agreement No 101119437). From 2024 to 2028, eight beneficiaries and ten associated partners, coordinated by the University of Bergen, train ten doctoral researchers to model and forecast coastal hazards, river and estuary dynamics, urban flooding and water quality, combining advanced numerical methods, GPU computing, laboratory experiments and field work.

Free to reuse in articles and partner communications.
