Opportunities

Open positions, calls for participation and other ways to get involved with the network.

Open positions

There are currently no open positions in RESCUER. All ten doctoral positions were filled in 2024. Future opportunities (for example, short courses and schools open to external PhD students) will be announced here and on our LinkedIn page.

Open training events

Some RESCUER training activities have been open to PhD candidates from outside the network. Workshop 2, for instance, welcomed doctoral candidates from the University of Bergen, the University of Zaragoza and the University of Pavia. If you are interested in joining a future event, contact us.

Recruitment archive

The original 2023–2024 calls for the ten doctoral projects are described on each fellow's profile. In line with MSCA rules, the positions were advertised internationally, including on EURAXESS.
