Mid-term meeting

May 12, 2025 Senigallia, Italy

The mid-term meeting with the EU Program Officer reviewed the network's progress roughly fifteen months into the project. After a tour de table of the beneficiaries and the Program Officer's presentation, the doctoral candidates introduced themselves and their projects in a session chaired by Michele Mossa. The coordinator reported on recruitment, the data management plan, intellectual property and the promotional video, followed by a summary of the fellows' feedback and restricted sessions with the fellows and with the coordinator. One outcome was a series of monthly informal meetings where fellows update each other.
