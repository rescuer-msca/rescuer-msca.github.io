HIC 2026 special session: Forecasting coastal and river hazards, flooding and water quality

June 23, 2026 Zaragoza, Spain

RESCUER organized a special session at the 16th International Conference on Hydroinformatics (HIC 2026) in Zaragoza: "Forecasting coastal and river hazards, flooding and water quality" (Topic 12). Proposed by Rita F. Carvalho, Claes Eskilsson, Maria Kazolea and Henrik Kalisch, it ran on Tuesday, June 23, 2026, in three blocks with ten talks on urban flooding, estuaries, wave propagation and water quality. Two RESCUER fellows, Alessandro Del Piero and Panu Pietikäinen, presented their PhD work in the session, and UNIVPM presented the Senigallia pilot site; four more fellows presented in other sessions of the conference, with a fifth as co-author.

Programme

All talks in Room 1.05, Betancourt Building (EINA). Talks by RESCUER fellows and partners are in bold.

Block 12.1 · Tuesday, June 23 · 12:40–13:40 · chair: Rita F. Carvalho

  • 12:40 Exploring large eddy simulations of laboratory-modelled flooding in a synthetic urban district — Saeed Hashemikia, Greet Deruyter, Tom De Mulder
  • 12:55 A phase-adaptive multi-objective evacuation optimisation framework integrating hydraulic simulation and agent-based modelling for urban flooding — Hao-Ming Hsu, Chang-Ying Lee, Ming-Che Hu
  • 13:10 Subcell finite volume techniques for robust wet/dry fronts in DG schemes — Alessandro Del Piero (DC7), Maria Kazolea, Andrea Gilberto Filippini
  • 13:25 Assessing the fecal-contamination contribution of sewer sources to downstream water quality with inverse transport modeling in the Seine River — Yoann Cartier et al.

Block 12.2 · Tuesday, June 23 · 15:45–16:45 · chair: Volker Roeber

  • 15:45 Integrating flood-risk mapping with transport accessibility analysis for mobility simulations during critical events — Thomas Cochrane, Lea Dasallas, Barry Evans, Markus Pahlow
  • 16:00 The influence of an offshore sediment depot on infragravity wave propagation up the Adour River — Panu Pietikäinen (DC3), Matthias Delpey, Denis Morichon
  • 16:15 PronUy_RPFM: a modular architecture for prototype development in operational forecasting of the Río de la Plata and its maritime front — Diego Silva Piedra et al.
  • 16:30 Sea–river interactions in the microtidal estuary of the Misa River (Central Italy), the RESCUER pilot site — Matteo Postacchini, Eleonora Perugini, Lorenzo Melito, Giovanna Darvini, Maurizio Brocchini

Block 12.3 · Tuesday, June 23 · 17:15–17:45 · chair: Volker Roeber

  • 17:15 Predicting nitrous oxide emission from China’s waterbodies with multiple deep-learning algorithms — Xihua Wang, Qinya Lv, Y. Jun Xu
  • 17:30 A hybrid metamodel for real-time prediction of compound flooding in estuarine environments — Jared Ortiz-Angulo et al.

RESCUER talks in other sessions

  • Tuesday 10:30 · Session 4.1, high-performance computing · Accelerating coastal modelling with the performance-portable BOSZ model — Víctor Loras Herrero (DC10) et al.
  • Tuesday 17:15 · Session 7.2, environmental and coastal hydroinformatics · 2D and 3D model applications for estuarine temperature and salinity simulation — Mengyun Wang (DC9), Adhemar Romero (DC5) et al.
  • Wednesday 10:15 · Session 7.3 · Splitting mode for well-mixed estuaries — Laure Sicard (DC6) et al.
  • Wednesday 17:15 · Session 4.5 · Step transition for plane waves at a free surface — Yago Pego Martínez (DC4), Henrik Kalisch
  • Thursday 12:15 · Session 7.5 · GPU-accelerated vertical multi-layer suspended sediment transport model for hyper-turbid events in estuaries — Laure Sicard (DC6) et al.

The conference itself (June 22–26, 2026) was chaired by RESCUER partner Pilar García-Navarro (University of Zaragoza). Times and rooms are from the official programme; the abstracts are in the conference’s book of abstracts.
