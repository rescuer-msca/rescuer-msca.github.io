Training workshop

Workshop 1: Numerical methods and wave modeling

December 2–6, 2024 Bergen, Norway

The network's first training workshop was a week-long, hands-on course in Bergen focused on wave modeling and numerical analysis. Morning lectures covered the water-wave problem, existence and stability of wave models, Boussinesq theory and linear wave theory, while the numerical track went from ODE time-stepping and Runge–Kutta stability to finite differences with summation-by-parts (SBP-SAT) boundary treatment and artificial diffusion for Burgers' equation. Afternoons were coding sessions with daily assignments. Fellows also presented their career goals and elected two representatives to the supervisory board.

Lecturers & speakers

  • Volker Roeber

    Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour (UPPA)

    Free-surface wave modeling: mathematical background, modeling considerations and numerical techniques for nearshore wave models; review of shallow-water, Boussinesq and spectral models, with applications and exercises

  • Magnus Svärd

    University of Bergen

    Numerical theory for evolution equations: stability, consistency and convergence; ODE time-stepping, Runge–Kutta methods, SBP-SAT finite differences, Burgers' equation and artificial diffusion

  • Henrik Kalisch

    University of Bergen

    Mathematical theory of wave models: existence, uniqueness and stability; the water-wave problem

  • Andreas

    Surf zone circulation (short talk)

  • Anders

    Nwogu equations (short talk)

Practical information

As announced to the participants before the workshop.

Venues

  • Workshop: a seminar room of the Grand Hotel Terminus, Zander Kaaes gate 6, 5015 Bergen.
  • Accommodation: Zander K Hotel, Zander Kaaes gate 8, 5015 Bergen (next door). Check-in December 1, 2024 from 3 p.m.; check-out December 7 by 11 a.m.

Dinners

Both dinners were part of the programme.

  • Welcome dinner, Monday, December 2, at Fløirestauranten on top of Mount Fløyen. Participants met at 5:15 p.m. in the lobby of the Grand Hotel Terminus and walked together to the Fløibanen funicular.
  • Farewell dinner, Thursday, December 5, at the Grand Hotel Terminus.

What to bring

A laptop with charger and the required software pre-installed, since every afternoon was a coding session. And clothes for Bergen’s December weather.

The workshop was organized by Henrik Kalisch (project coordinator) and Rosa Maria Vargas-Magaña (then project manager).
