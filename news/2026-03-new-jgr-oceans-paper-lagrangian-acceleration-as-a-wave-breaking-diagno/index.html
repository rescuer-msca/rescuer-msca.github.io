ResearchMarch 5, 2026

New JGR Oceans paper: Lagrangian acceleration as a wave-breaking diagnostic

A paper led by Rosa Maria Vargas-Magaña (University of Bergen), with Henrik Kalisch and co-authors, came out in the Journal of Geophysical Research: Oceans.

It proposes Lagrangian acceleration as a diagnostic for wave breaking in the nearshore zone. The co-authors include Marc Buckley, Maria Bjørnestad, Tessa Gronemann, Karoline Holand, Jochen Horstmann, Michael Streßer and Sébastien Fromenteau. The paper is one of the recent network publications listed in Newsletter #4, and Henrik Kalisch’s announcement of it was reshared on the RESCUER LinkedIn page.

Bergen, Norway
