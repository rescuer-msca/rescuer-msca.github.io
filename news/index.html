NetworkMay 13, 2025

Workshop 3 takes the fellows to the Senigallia pilot site

Workshop 3 followed the midterm meeting at the Hotel Raffaello in Senigallia on 13-14 May 2025. It had three units: environmental statistics, taught by Francesco Lagona (Roma Tre); identification and characterization of urban flood drifters, taught by Mário J. Franca (KIT), including a walk to risk hotspots in the city center; and field measurement techniques, taught by Matteo Postacchini, Lorenzo Melito and Maurizio Brocchini (UNIVPM).

FellowsFebruary 1, 2025

Meet the ten RESCUER fellows

The second newsletter introduced the network's doctoral fellows. They are Víctor Loras Herrero (UPPA, surf-zone pollutant transport), Alessandro Del Piero (Inria, urban flood modeling), Laure Sicard and Mengyun Wang (UNIZAR, river and estuary models; urban water quality), Panu Pietikäinen (SUEZ-RPT/UPPA, operational wave forecasting) and Yago Pego Martínez (UiB, ocean swell in fjords).

AnnouncementFebruary 1, 2025

One year of RESCUER: all deliverables on time and first publications

In February 2025 RESCUER marked the end of its first year. The second newsletter reports that all scheduled deliverables were completed on time, and that work was moving on to the secondment plans and each fellow's career development plan.

First RESCUER training workshop in Bergen

First RESCUER newsletter published

Recruitment nearly complete: seven fellows on board

By November 2024, recruitment of the ten doctoral fellows was almost finished. Seven fellows had already started, one was due to begin on 1 December 2024, and the last two were expected in 2025.

AnnouncementApril 8, 2024

Ten fully funded RESCUER PhD positions open

In spring 2024 RESCUER advertised ten fully funded, 36-month PhD positions across eight host organizations: UiB, Aalborg University, SUEZ-Rivages Pro Tech, the University of Coimbra, the University of Zaragoza, Inria at the University of Bordeaux, UNIVPM and UPPA.

NetworkMarch 25, 2024

RESCUER kicks off in Bergen

The consortium met for the kick-off meeting at the University of Bergen's Realfagbygget on 25-26 March 2024, after an icebreaker on 24 March.

AnnouncementFebruary 1, 2024

RESCUER Doctoral Network officially starts

RESCUER (Resilient Solutions for Coastal, Urban, Estuarine and Riverine Environments) officially began on 1 February 2024 as a Horizon Europe Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network (grant 101119437), coordinated by the University of Bergen and running until 31 January 2028.
