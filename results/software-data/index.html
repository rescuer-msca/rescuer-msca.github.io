Open science

Software & data

RESCUER is a computational network: our results live in code and data as much as in papers. Here we share them.

Open by default

As a Horizon Europe project, RESCUER follows the programme's open-science rules. Peer-reviewed publications are deposited in trusted repositories with immediate open access, and research data are managed according to the FAIR principles (findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable): as open as possible, as closed as necessary.

Training materials are shared openly too: codes from our workshops are published on GitHub and use free, open-source tools.

  • Open-access papersDeposited in trusted repositories.
  • FAIR dataFindable, accessible, interoperable, reusable.
  • Open codeVersioned, documented and citable.

Software

Numerical models and tools developed, extended or used in RESCUER, and why each one matters for the network.

AeroSol

Inria

C++ finite-element library for advection–diffusion problems with arbitrarily high-order discretizations on hybrid triangle/quadrilateral meshes, designed for high-performance computing.

In RESCUER: The library underneath UHAINA, used for DC7's code development and tests (deliverable D3.1).

partner-code

Website

BOSZ (Boussinesq Ocean and Surf Zone model)

UPPA

Phase-resolving Boussinesq-type model for nearshore waves, surf-zone processes and run-up, developed in the KOSTARISK joint laboratory (UPPA, SUEZ Rivages Pro Tech, AZTI). BOSZ is not yet publicly released.

In RESCUER: One of the network's core nearshore wave models (WP1 and WP4), developed further within RESCUER by DC10 Víctor Loras Herrero. A one-dimensional version runs live in your browser in the Wave lab.

partner-code

Try it in the Wave lab

Ocean Wave Tracing v1

Python solver of the wave-ray equations for ocean waves over variable depth and currents (Halsne et al., 2023).

In RESCUER: Used by DC3 Panu Pietikäinen to trace how waves are refracted and focused by the Capbreton submarine canyon and the South Aquitaine bathymetry, before running the phase-resolving model (deliverable D1.1, which cites Halsne et al., 2023).

OpenFOAM

UC

Open-source computational fluid dynamics (CFD) toolbox.

In RESCUER: Used at the University of Coimbra to reproduce dye-tracer flume experiments of a stretch of the Mondego River (Newsletter 3), and by DC9 Mengyun Wang and DC5 Adhemar Romero for 3D salinity modeling in the Mondego estuary (Wang et al., 2026).

RiverFlow2D

Hydronia Europe

Commercial two-dimensional flexible-mesh finite-volume model for river hydraulics, flooding and sediment transport. Its GPU engine was developed with the University of Zaragoza's Computational Hydraulics Group.

In RESCUER: The flood model of associated partner Hydronia Europe. DC6 Laure Sicard will work with Hydronia to apply her river and estuary model in a realistic, operational context (Newsletter 4).

partner-code

License: Proprietary (commercial) Website

UHAINA

Inria

Phase-resolving free-surface model for coastal engineering, built on the AeroSol finite-element library. It solves a weakly dispersive, fully nonlinear Green–Naghdi system on unstructured meshes.

In RESCUER: The code base in which DC7 Alessandro Del Piero implements his sub-cell discontinuous Galerkin treatment of wet/dry fronts for urban flooding (WP3, deliverable D3.1).

partner-code

Website

w2rescuer (Workshop 2 materials)

UNIZAR

Codes and exercises of the online Workshop 2 (March 6–19, 2025), with one folder per day of the course.

In RESCUER: RESCUER teaching material from the University of Zaragoza: 1D and 2D shallow-water models, sediment transport, urban flows and GPU programming.

WavePropError

UPPA

C++ code for the wave-propagation error analysis of the one-dimensional Nwogu equations.

In RESCUER: Released with Mihami & Roeber (2026, Coastal Engineering), a RESCUER-funded study of the numerical schemes behind Boussinesq-type models such as BOSZ.

Senigallia / Misa River estuary monitoring data

UNIVPM

Field data from the Misa River estuary and the Senigallia coast: a stream gauge about 1 km upstream of the mouth, an acoustic profiler 4 km offshore, a harbor tide gauge, a video-monitoring station (since 2015), an X-band radar, ADCPs, turbidity sensors and drifters.

In RESCUER: Data from the RESCUER pilot site (WP3, deliverable D3.3), used to calibrate and validate the consortium's river, estuary and coastal models.
