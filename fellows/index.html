What does a RESCUER fellow do?

Each fellow works on an individual research project with a main supervisor and co-supervisors from academia and industry, takes part in the network's training workshops, and spends research stays (secondments) at other partners. Fellows are employed by their host institution under the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions rules.
