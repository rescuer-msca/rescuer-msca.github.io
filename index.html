Our mission

A new generation of researchers for water-related hazards

Coastal communities face growing risks from storm waves, river floods, urban flooding and pollution.

RESCUER (Resilient Solutions for Coastal, Urban, Estuarine and Riverine Environments) builds on a decade of advances in the numerical treatment of multi-scale and multi-physics problems. Our fellows turn these advances into practical models of hydrodynamic phenomena in the coastal and estuarine zone, with one goal: improving the overall safety of coastal areas.

Each fellow is enrolled in a doctoral program, works with academic and industrial supervisors, and spends secondments at other partners across the network.

About the project

Research

Four connected research themes

From waves at the open coast to pollutants in city sewers: the work packages follow water through the whole system.

All research
WP1

Coastal hazards

Wave-by-wave models of the nearshore zone for operational forecasting of run-up, overtopping and coastal flooding.

WP2

River & estuary dynamics

Fast, accurate models of flows over erodible beds, coupled with groundwater and sediment transport.

WP3

Urban flooding

High-resolution flood models for cities, including buildings, street furniture, cars and floating debris, validated in the laboratory.

WP4

Water quality

Tracking pollutants, bacteria, sediments and plastics from cities and rivers to estuaries and the surf zone.
