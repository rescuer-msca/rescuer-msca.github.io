Training

Training & events

RESCUER fellows learn together: week-long workshops across Europe, online courses, field and lab days, and secondments with industry and public authorities.

Programme

Four strands of training

Every doctoral candidate combines individual research with shared, network-wide training.

Network-wide workshops

Thematic workshops and network meetings, hosted in turn by the partners, in person and online. Mornings are for lectures, afternoons for hands-on coding and lab sessions.

The doctoral project

Each fellow is enrolled in a PhD program at their host institution and works on an individual research project with a main supervisor and co-supervisors.

Secondments

Mandatory research stays of 3–6 months at other partners, at least one of them outside academia, where fellows work on practical coastal, river and urban-flooding problems.

Transferable skills

Research software practice, open science, academic integrity and publishing, communication, career development plans, intellectual property and data management.

Curriculum

What the fellows learn

Scientific topics taught at the network events so far, grouped by research theme.

Foundations

  • Mathematical theory of water-wave models
  • Numerical theory for evolution equations, Runge–Kutta and SBP-SAT schemes
  • High-order schemes for shallow-water equations

WP1 · Coastal

  • Free-surface wave modeling: shallow-water, Boussinesq and spectral models
  • Linear wave theory
  • Surf-zone circulation

WP2 · River & estuary

  • 1D and 2D hydraulic models and mesh generation
  • Erodible beds and sediment transport
  • Estuary field observations (Mondego estuary)

WP3 · Urban

  • Porosity models for urban flooding
  • Urban flood drifters: identification and characterization
  • Field measurements at the Senigallia pilot site

WP4 · Water quality

  • Water chemical characterization and lab tests
  • Physical modeling in hydraulics
  • Particle image velocimetry (PIV)

Computing

  • GPU programming: CUDA, Kokkos, SYCL, OpenMP
  • HPC architectures and parallel computing
  • Code coupling and co-simulation (MPI, preCICE, MUI)
  • Python scientific programming and digital mapping

Secondments

Learning outside the home lab

Secondments last 3–6 months with full pay. Each fellow spends time at an academic and a non-academic partner, where they are exposed to a different working environment and to practical problems in coastal protection, river hydraulics and urban flooding.

Non-academic hosts include Águas da Figueira (Portugal), AZTI (Spain), the Norwegian Public Roads Administration, the civil protection of the Municipality of Senigallia (Italy), Hydronia Europe (Spain) and COWI (Denmark).

Open teaching materials
