About

Team & governance

RESCUER is run by its partners together: a coordination team at the University of Bergen, supervisors at every host organization, and boards in which the fellows have a voice.

Coordination team

The coordination team manages the network day to day: science, training, finances, reporting and communication.

Project coordinator

Henrik Kalisch

Professor of Applied Mathematics; Deputy Head of the Department of Mathematics

University of Bergen

Henrik Kalisch is Professor of Applied Mathematics at the University of Bergen, where his research centers on mathematical modeling of nearshore processes such as wave breaking, surf-zone circulation and coastal wave hazards; he received his PhD from the University of Texas at Austin in 2001 and is co-editor-in-chief of 'Water Waves'.

Profile of Henrik Kalisch

Deputy coordinator

Matteo Postacchini

Associate Professor

Università Politecnica delle Marche

Matteo Postacchini specializes in maritime, fluvial and urban flooding hydraulics, combining numerical modeling of shallow flows with laboratory research on wave propagation and human stability in floods, and has carried out numerous field studies along the Misa River and its estuary in Senigallia.

Project manager

Anders Norevik

Researcher, Department of Mathematics

University of Bergen

Anders Norevik is a researcher at the University of Bergen who completed his PhD in 2025 with a dissertation on conservation laws, particle paths and wave breaking in long-wave models, supervised in Henrik Kalisch's group.

Profile of Anders Norevik

Maria Kazolea

Inria, Centre Inria de l'université de Bordeaux

Maria Kazolea is an Inria research scientist in the CARDAMOM team in Bordeaux whose work focuses on efficient numerical modeling of free-surface and nonlinear waves, Boussinesq-type models and high-order schemes for shallow-water flows.

Andrea Gilberto Filippini

Research engineer

BRGM (French Geological Survey)

Andrea Gilberto Filippini has been a research engineer at BRGM, the French Geological Survey, since 2018, working on coastal risks. He received his PhD from the University of Bordeaux in 2016 (Inria, supervised by Mario Ricchiuto and Philippe Bonneton) on free-surface flow simulation in estuarine and coastal environments on unstructured meshes. Governance

How the network is run

Decisions in RESCUER are shared between the partners and the fellows.

The network's committees and the data management plan were set up at the kick-off meeting in Bergen in March 2024. Progress is reviewed with the European Research Executive Agency (REA), for example at the mid-term meeting in Senigallia in May 2025.

Meet the fellows

  • Supervisory board

    The network's main decision-making body, with representatives of the partner organizations. At Workshop 1 in December 2024, the fellows elected two representatives to sit on the board.

  • Coordination team

    Led by the coordinator at the University of Bergen, with a deputy coordinator, a project manager and a communications lead. It handles scientific coordination, project management and reporting to the EU.

  • Work package leaders

    Each research theme and the cross-cutting work on training, dissemination and exploitation is led by a partner. SUEZ Rivages Pro Tech manages exploitation and intellectual property for the project.

  • Fellows' meetings

    Since the mid-term meeting, the fellows hold monthly informal meetings where they update each other on their progress.
