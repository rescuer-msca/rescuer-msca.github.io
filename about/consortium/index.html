About

Consortium

18 organizations in 8 countries: universities, research institutes, companies and public authorities working together on coastal, river and urban water challenges.

  • Beneficiary (8)
  • Associated partner (10)

Beneficiaries

Beneficiaries sign the grant agreement with the European Union, employ the doctoral candidates and enroll them in their doctoral programs.

🇳🇴

UiB

University of Bergen

University · Bergen, Norway

Project coordinator and project management

Department of Mathematics

Norway's second-largest public research university, located in Bergen. Its Department of Mathematics contributes expertise in partial differential equation modeling of free-surface flows and the design of numerical methods.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC2 · Vassilis Georgopoulos
  • DC4 · Yago Pego Martínez
Website
🇩🇰

AAU

Aalborg University

University · Aalborg, Denmark

Hosts a doctoral candidate

Department of the Built Environment, Ocean and Coastal Engineering group

A Danish public university with its main campus in Aalborg. Its Department of the Built Environment includes an ocean and coastal engineering group working on wave modeling and coupled simulations.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC1 · Gábor Albrecht
Website
🇫🇷

Inria

Inria, Centre Inria de l’université de Bordeaux

Research organization · Talence, France

Hosts a doctoral candidate; leads WP6 (dissemination)

CARDAMOM project-team

France's national research institute for digital science and technology. The CARDAMOM team at its Bordeaux center develops high-order numerical methods for free-surface and coastal flows, including the UHAINA wave model built on the AeroSol library.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC7 · Alessandro Del Piero
Website
🇫🇷

SUEZ-RPT

SUEZ Eau France – Rivages Pro Tech

Company · Bidart, France

Hosts a doctoral candidate; exploitation and IP

Rivages Pro Tech, Technopole Izarbel, Bidart

Rivages Pro Tech is a SUEZ Eau France center, created in 2014 in the French Basque Country, that develops and operates operational oceanography and early-warning services for bathing-water quality and coastal hazards.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC3 · Panu Pietikäinen
Website
🇫🇷

UPPA

Université de Pau et des Pays de l'Adour

University · Anglet, France

Hosts DC10 and directs the thesis of DC3

SIAME laboratory, Anglet campus (E2S UPPA HPC-Waves chair)

A public university in southwestern France with campuses in Pau and on the Basque coast. Its SIAME laboratory in Anglet develops phase-resolving wave models and studies coastal hazards along the Basque coast.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC10 · Víctor Loras Herrero
Website
🇮🇹

UNIVPM

Università Politecnica delle Marche

University · Ancona, Italy

Deputy coordinator

Department of Civil and Building Engineering, and Architecture (DICEA)

A public technical university in Ancona on Italy's Adriatic coast. Its hydraulics group combines laboratory wave-flume experiments, field monitoring and numerical modeling of coastal, estuarine and urban flows.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC8 · Bekan Chelkeba Tumsa
Website
🇵🇹

UC

University of Coimbra

University · Coimbra, Portugal

Hosts a doctoral candidate

Department of Civil Engineering and MARE / ARNET

One of Europe's oldest universities, founded in 1290 and based in Coimbra, Portugal. Its Department of Civil Engineering conducts hydraulics research combining laboratory flumes, CFD and field data for rivers and estuaries.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC5 · Adhemar Romero
Website
🇪🇸

UNIZAR

University of Zaragoza

University · Zaragoza, Spain

Leads WP2 (river and estuary dynamics)

Computational Hydraulics Group (GHC) and Fluid Dynamics Technologies (TFD), Aragon Institute of Engineering Research (I3A)

A public university founded in 1542 and the largest higher-education center in the Ebro Valley. Its Computational Hydraulics Group develops GPU-accelerated shallow-water models for floods, sediment transport and environmental flows.

Doctoral candidates hosted

  • DC6 · Laure Sicard
  • DC9 · Mengyun Wang
Website

UBx

Associated partner

Civil Protection

The municipality of Senigallia, a coastal town on the central Adriatic in the Marche region, built on a low-lying plain around the mouth of the Misa River.

Website

University · Rome, Italy

Associated partner and secondment host

Associated partner and secondment host

Águas da Figueira

Company · Figueira da Foz, Portugal

Associated partner and potential secondment host (non-academic sector)

Website

AZTI

AZTI

Research organization · Pasaia, Spain

Associated partner

Pasaia site (operational oceanography)

A Basque technology center specializing in marine and food research. Its operational oceanography team contributes metocean data, video monitoring and field deployments for coastal-risk studies.

Website

Hydronia Europe

Company · Madrid, Spain

Associated partner and secondment host
